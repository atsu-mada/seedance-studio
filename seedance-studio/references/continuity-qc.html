Continuity QC

Use this reference before finalizing a sequence prompt and after reviewing a generated take.

Hard-Fail Without Explanation

Do not silently change:

  • canonical identity;
  • wardrobe;
  • product identity;
  • product geometry;
  • prop ownership;
  • location;
  • vehicle identity;
  • persistent environment;
  • exact reference tag;
  • accepted completed beat status;
  • parent clip lineage.

Warn Unless Declared

Warn on unexplained changes in pose, frame position, screen direction, motion vector, camera phase, focus state, lighting phase, emotional state, ambience, music phase, or active dialogue.

Allow changes only when declared under transition, allowed changes, accepted deviation, intentional axis reset, or intentional next shot.

Spatial Anchor Continuity (technique T3) [field]

One physical object stays one object in one place. When the camera moves, the object's screen position changes, but it must not be re-introduced from somewhere else in the world, duplicated, or swapped for a new copy.

  • Name the anchor once with its world position: the lighthouse stays on the headland to the left of the boat for the whole clip.
  • Describe camera motion relative to the anchor: as the camera pans right with the boat, the lighthouse slides left and out of frame. Do not describe the anchor as moving when only the camera moves.
  • If the anchor leaves frame and must return, return it where the camera path puts it, not where the composition would look best.
  • Review check: follow the anchor frame by frame across camera moves and cuts; a jump to a new world position is a hard fail unless it is declared.

Boundary Check

Before a successor prompt ships, verify:

  • predecessor accepted status;
  • successor parent_clip_id;
  • predecessor observed_end_state;
  • successor planned_start_state;
  • completed beats excluded;
  • future beats excluded;
  • exact tags preserved;
  • current prompt covers only current clip.
