Emotional beat performance (technique T6) [field]

Symptom: in extreme close-ups and tight close-ups, faces tend to come back neutral and still: a blank stare that reads as eerie rather than moved. The tighter the frame, the more a missing performance shows.

Repair: give each beat its own expression, not one mood word for the whole clip. Name the emotion, one visible micro-action, and how it changes by the end of the beat. Keep one change per beat so the face has time to play it.

0–3s: extreme close-up, her eyes widen slightly and her breath catches, lips parting. 3–6s: her brows soften, eyes glisten, and a small unsure smile starts at one corner of the mouth. Ends on the smile held, gaze still on the light.

Pair with the shot-size cautions: close shots need a small, specific action, not a large one.

Proxy-shape transfer from previz (technique T5) [field]

Symptom: symbols used in previz or blocking references (glowing spheres for characters, boxes for buildings, flat unlit colors, grid floors) show up literally in the generated video.

Cause: a video reference with no narrow role transfers appearance along with motion.

Repair: give the previz reference one role (camera path, timing, and subject positions only) and write the real form in plain words next to it: the figures remain human-shaped, not glowing orbs; the buildings are brick houses, not grey boxes. Put identity and look in image references or text. See the previz-driven R2V structure.
