Moderation-Safe Rewording (technique T8) [field]

Benign scenes are sometimes blocked because a verb describes physical contact, penetration, or destruction, even when the event is gentle or symbolic. Keep the event and its meaning; replace only the physical verb with a soft visual metaphor that shows what the viewer should actually see. This is a clarity repair for a misread benign prompt. It is not a way to disguise harmful content, and it does not apply to scenes that are actually violent, sexual, or harmful.

Verb classRisky wordingGentle metaphor
Penetration / entering a bodythe light enters his body · 光が彼の体に入り込むthe light overlaps his silhouette and melts into him · 光が彼の輪郭に重なり、溶け込んでいく
Destructionthe tower collapses and explodes · 塔が崩れ落ち、爆発するthe tower unravels into drifting, glowing paper confetti · 塔がほどけて、光る紙吹雪になって舞い上がる
Forceful contactthe wave smashes into the girl · 波が少女に叩きつけられるthe wave washes over the girl and passes, leaving her standing · 波が少女を包み込み、そのまま通り過ぎていく
  • Describe the visible result (overlap, dissolve, drift, wrap) instead of the physical act (pierce, enter, break, hit).
  • Change one clause per retry, so the blocked phrase can be found.
  • Field observation (2026-09, one surface): a blocked job was not charged and passed after one such rewording. Charging and moderation behavior are surface-specific; the operator skill for the active suite owns the retry procedure.

Multilingual Clarity

Language mixing is allowed when it makes a safe prompt clearer:

  • Keep technical camera terms in English when they are more precise: locked medium shot, 35mm lens, slow dolly-in.
  • Keep Chinese role binding when it is shorter: @图1锁定主体身份, @视频1仅参考运镜.
  • Keep dialogue in the speaker language, and keep safety/ownership constraints explicit.
  • Do not translate unsafe content into another language as a workaround.
  • If a prompt still fails, change the risky creative surface instead of only changing language.
