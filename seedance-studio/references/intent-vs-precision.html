Intent vs Precision

Seedance prompting is not pixel scripting. The text should direct intent; references should carry dense visual information.

Use Text For

  • action;
  • emotional intent;
  • camera behavior;
  • timing;
  • light changes;
  • audio;
  • constraints.

Use References For

  • identity;
  • product design;
  • motion examples;
  • camera rhythm;
  • voice or sound texture;
  • material appearance.

Short prompts should be dense, not vague.
